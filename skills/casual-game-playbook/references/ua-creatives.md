# UA Creatives and Channels

## Hook matrix

Make one gameplay capture, then wrap it in many emotional hooks. Fill `templates/creative-matrix.md` with 6–12 combinations of hook × art theme.

| Hook family | Example headline pattern | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Audience call-out | "Designed for [audience]" / "Made for grown-ups" | Narrowing to high-LTV segments |
| Ego / brain test | "Your brain age: 40?" / "Only 2% solve level 50" | Number, word and logic puzzles |
| Challenge | "Harder than you think!" / "Can you find it?" | Hidden object, sort, escape |
| Relaxation | "So relaxing" / "Take a 5-minute break" | Cozy, zen positioning |
| Use moment | "Make your commute shorter" / "Unwind before bed" | Session-based casual |
| Mental sharpness | "Keep your mind sharp" | Older audiences |
| Direct CTA | "Try [Game] today!" | Retargeting, brand |
| Satisfaction | "So satisfying" + clean sort or clear animation | Sort and merge games |

## Video recipe (15–30 s, vertical 9:16)

1. **0–2 s:** headline hook in bold text + the most visual moment.
2. **2–10 s:** a hand cursor plays real gameplay. Show the progress counter and the target-item bar.
3. **10–15 s:** a near-success or celebration burst ("AWESOME!").
4. **End card:** icon, name and Install / GET.

Rules:
- Real gameplay from the actual build only.
- Make 2–4 near-duplicate variants of each concept (headline, colour, first frame) so the ad network's algorithm can optimise.
- Swap art themes on the same mechanic (jungle, beach, winter, cosy home) to find the winning skin.
- AI tools are fine for backgrounds and variants, but the gameplay footage must be real.

## Channels (in order of use)

| Channel | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google App Campaigns (UAC) | First paid channel | Covers YouTube, Play, Search and AdMob. Upload 10–20 videos + text. Optimise for installs first, then in-app action or tROAS once data exists |
| Meta (Facebook / Instagram) | After Google works | Strong for 35+ women. Use the Advantage+ app campaign |
| AppLovin / Unity / Mintegral / Moloco | Scale stage | Programmatic in-app networks. Need playable ads and tROAS bidding |
| Apple Search Ads | iOS winners | Bid on genre keywords and competitor-free terms |
| TikTok | Optional | Younger skew, may fit less with 35+ audiences |
| Cross-promo (own games) | Once 2+ live games | Free installs between franchise siblings |

## Budget pacing

- Test: $50–$100/day per creative set until about 100 installs per variant.
- Scale: raise the daily budget by 20–30% at most every 3–4 days, while D7 ROAS holds.
- Refresh: about 20% of the creatives each week. Kill any creative whose IPM drops 30% from its peak.

## Geo strategy

- Tier-1 English (US, UK, CA, AU, NZ) for monetization and scale.
- Cheap geos (IN, PH, LATAM) only for early retention signals and creative screening.
- US iOS is usually the most valuable single market for casual puzzle with older audiences.
